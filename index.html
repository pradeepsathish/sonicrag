Continuous Acoustic Signal Processing & Feature Engineering
A complete architectural breakdown of how raw 44.1kHz audio streams are ingested, normalized, decomposed into multi-scale time-frequency representations, and projected into cross-modal embedding spaces from absolute scratch.
1. Ingestion Standardization & Psychoacoustic Pre-Processing

Before computing spectral transforms, raw input audio undergoes strict broadcast-standard normalization to eliminate loudness bias and phase cancellation:

Equal-Energy Downmix

Stereo channels converted via pan law: \(M = \frac{L + R}{\sqrt{2}}\) ensuring center-image conservation without clipping.

Loudness Normalization

Standardized to \(-14\text{ LUFS}\) integrated loudness (EBU R128 standard) with true peak ceiling at \(-1.0\text{ dBFS}\).

Sliding Window Chunking

Segmented into \(10.0\text{s}\) rolling frames with \(50\%\) overlap (\(5.0\text{s}\) hop size) with symmetric reflection padding.

4. Temporal Envelope & Onset Rhythmics

To quantify rhythmic pocket, groove, and tempo drift, the system extracts the half-wave rectified spectral flux:

Spectral Flux Onset Curve

\(\text{SF}(t) = \sum_k \max(0, |X(t, k)| - |X(t-1, k)|)\) tracks transient strike velocity.

Autocorrelation Tempogram

Evaluates tempo across lag windows \([40, 240]\text{ BPM}\), disambiguating half/double-time.

Syncopation & Micro-Timing

Measures swing ratio and sub-millisecond deviation from quantized 16th-note grids.

5. Cross-Modal Embedding Space (CLAP Dual-Encoder)

How natural language and raw waveforms meet in a unified 512-dimensional metric space:

Text Branch: HTSAT-Unfused Transformer

Tokenizes natural language queries up to 77 tokens, passing through a 12-layer transformer to yield a 768-dim context representation, projected into \(\vec{u} \in \mathbb{R}^{512}\) with \(L_2\) normalization.

Audio Branch: Hierarchical Swin-Transformer

Processes the \(128\times 1000\) Mel-spectrogram patches through shifted window attention across 4 hierarchical stages, producing \(\vec{v} \in \mathbb{R}^{512}\) with \(L_2\) normalization.

Distributed Ingestion Pipeline

Incoming audio is queued via a distributed Kafka topic (audio-ingest-stream). Ray worker pools process rolling 10s chunks asynchronously, running GPU-accelerated DSP extraction and CLAP audio embedding generation in parallel batches of 32.

HNSW Vector Sharding Topology

Vectors are sharded into in-memory clusters partitioned by BPM tempo ranges and root keys. Each node maintains a 512-dim index (\(M=32, ef=128\)), keeping the RAM footprint at \(\approx 2.1\text{GB}\) per 1M vectors.

Active-Learning Creator Feedback Flywheel

Searches where users immediately skip candidates or re-phrase queries are automatically flagged as hard negatives. The system extracts the top-skipped audio stems and queues them for weekly contrastive adapter fine-tuning, progressively sharpening the model's understanding of complex, multi-adjective music prompts.
