An experiment in music search

Describe the sound in your head. Find it in seconds.

Most music search still runs on tags and titles. ALAI explores a different idea: what if you could just describe a feeling — a key, a mood, a tempo, a texture — and have it understand you? This is a working prototype of that idea, built from scratch.

The idea
How it works
Four steps, from a sentence to a ranked list of matching tracks.
01
Describe it
Type how the track should feel — key, tempo, mood, texture — in plain language.
→
02
Parse intent
The sentence is broken into musical signals: root note, mode, tempo, brightness.
→
03
Match against audio
Every track is pre-analyzed for the same signals, extracted directly from the waveform.
→
04
Rank & return
Tracks are scored and ordered by how closely they match what you described.
